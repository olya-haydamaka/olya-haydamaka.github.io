Open tools for artists

Small, free tools for the practical side of making art, from preparing prints to getting work ready for a studio. They run in your browser, need no account, and never upload your work.

Tools

Postcard fronts

Open toolSource

Place paintings on postcard fronts and backs, check bleed, trim and resolution, and export print-ready 300 dpi TIFF files for the print studio.

TypeScriptMITRuns in the browser

More tools will be added here as they are made.

How they're made

  • FreeMIT licence. Use them for personal or commercial work.
  • PrivateYour files stay on your computer. Nothing is uploaded or stored.
  • SimpleOne task per tool, no sign-up, works on any desktop browser.
